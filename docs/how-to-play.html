How to Play

Dual N-Back combines a position (one of 9 squares) and a letter (e.g. C, H, K) each turn. A match means: the current stimulus is the same as the one N steps back. Tap Position when the square matches; tap Audio when the letter matches. You can tap both if both match.

The grid

Each turn, a position appears in one of 9 squares (3×3 grid):

123 456 789

The highlighted square is an example of where the stimulus might appear. At the same time you hear a letter. Your job is to detect when either the position or the letter (or both) matches the one from N steps ago.

N=1: One step back

For N=1, a match means: same as the previous turn (1 step back).

  • Each turn you see a position (one of 9 squares) and hear a letter (C, H, K, …).
  • Tap Position (Visual Match) if the position is the same as last turn.
  • Tap Audio if the letter is the same as last turn.
  • You can tap both if both match. Don’t tap if neither matches.

N=2: Two steps back

For N=2, a match means: same as 2 turns ago (not the last turn).

  • Same as N=1: each turn = one position + one letter.
  • Tap Position if the position matches 2 turns ago.
  • Tap Audio if the letter matches 2 turns ago.
  • Tap both if both match; don’t tap if neither matches.

Higher N levels

At N=3 you match 3 steps back, and so on. The higher the N, the harder the task. In Dual N-Back Pro you can train at any N from 1 up to 15. We increase difficulty when you master the current level, and decrease when it’s too hard—so you stay in the flow.

During a session

Use Pause to pause, Resume to continue, and Quit to leave (you can resume later). At the end you’ll see your N-level, audio accuracy, visual accuracy, and overall accuracy. Your N-level may increase, decrease, or stay the same based on your performance.
