Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Date]

Dual N-Back (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Dual N-Back Pro app. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Data we collect

Account and sign-in. If you sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier (e.g. user ID) and, depending on the provider and your choices, may receive email address and display name. We use this to identify your account and to sync your data.

Progress and settings. The app stores your settings (e.g. theme, speed, daily reminder), your streak (e.g. last completed date, current streak), and your session history (e.g. date, N-level, accuracy). When you are signed in, this data is also stored in our cloud database (Firebase Firestore) so it can sync across your devices. When you use the app as a guest, this data is stored only on your device.

Notifications. If you enable the daily reminder, we store your reminder time locally. We do not send notification content to our servers.

How we use your data

We use the data above to provide the app (including account management, progress tracking, and cross-device sync), to improve the app, and to respond to support requests when you contact us.

Third parties

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and cloud storage. Account and progress data is stored in Firebase. Google’s privacy policy applies to their processing: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple ID settings apply: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Your choices and deleting data

You can sign out at any time from Settings. To delete your cloud data, use the “Reset app & data” option in Settings (this clears local and, for signed-in users, cloud data and returns the app to a fresh state). For account deletion or data requests, contact us at the email below.
