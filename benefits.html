Benefits of Dual N-Back Training

Dual N-Back is a working-memory task used in cognitive research. Training on it may support certain cognitive abilities. The information below is for general interest and is not medical or professional advice. For health or cognitive concerns, please consult a qualified professional.

Working memory

Working memory is the ability to hold and manipulate information briefly (e.g. a few seconds) while you use it. The N-Back task directly exercises this: you must keep the last N positions and letters in mind and compare them to the current stimulus. Regular practice may help improve working memory capacity and control.

Sustained attention

Performing the task requires focused attention over time. You must watch and listen each turn and decide whether there is a match. Training may support your ability to sustain attention and resist distraction during similar activities.

Dual-tasking

In Dual N-Back you process two streams at once—visual (position) and auditory (letter). Practicing this may help with tasks that require coordinating multiple streams of information or switching between them.

Transfer and fluid intelligence

Some studies have suggested that intensive N-Back training may correlate with improvements on tests of fluid intelligence (reasoning and novel problem-solving). Results in the literature vary, and more research is needed. See the Science page for citations.

Consistency matters

As with many cognitive and skill-based activities, benefits are often associated with consistent, progressive practice rather than one-off sessions. Dual N-Back Pro is designed to let you train at an appropriate difficulty (N level) and build a regular habit.
